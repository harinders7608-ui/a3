Privacy Policy

Transparent governance, student privacy protection, and computer science educational standards for DecodeLearnUp.

DecodeLearnUp operates our Manhattan laboratory and educational academy to teach algorithmic thinking, develop computer science curricula, and champion ethical technology literacy. We collect minimal digital telemetry and contact records strictly to coordinate student enrollments, manage lab workstations, and answer educational inquiries. Our academy complies fully with global data protection frameworks, never selling or leasing your private records to commercial marketing entities across digital channels.

When you explore our computer science learning modules, request bespoke institutional curricula, or subscribe to our algorithmic pedagogy bulletins, we collect basic technical records including your IP address and browser type. If you enroll in an algorithmic workshop or book an in-person coding mentorship session, we collect your name, postal address, email address, telephone contact, and payment details through encrypted gateways. All confidential student records remain protected within our secure New York data vault.

Our data processing operations rest upon legitimate educational delivery, contractual fulfillment for commissioned student enrollments, and your explicit consent for laboratory bulletins. We retain student academic records solely for the duration mandated by municipal educational standards and professional credential verification protocols. Anonymized browsing telemetry is analyzed periodically to optimize website performance, refine mobile drawer navigation, and maintain stable server operation across all devices.

We implement robust security safeguards including Secure Sockets Layer protocols, layered network firewalls, and restricted credentials to protect your submitted records. Access to student records is restricted strictly to authorized faculty members and systems administrators managing laboratory infrastructure and admissions logistics. While rigorous security protocols are maintained across our internal systems, internet transmissions carry inherent limitations, and students are advised to protect credentials.

Depending upon your place of residence, you hold recognized statutory rights concerning your personal records, including the right to inspect, correct, export, or request deletion of stored files. You may also object to automated data processing or revoke consent for academic dispatches at any time without penalty. To exercise these individual privacy rights, submit a formal written request directly to our academic governance council at our primary Mercer Street facility.

Our computational academy is committed to resolving all student data inquiries with complete transparency and prompt diligence. Please direct your formal privacy communications to Data Protection Officer, DecodeLearnUp Academy, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or telephone our support desk directly at +1-888-777-5845. We update this privacy policy periodically to reflect evolving data governance standards and instructional benchmarks across our worldwide learning network.
